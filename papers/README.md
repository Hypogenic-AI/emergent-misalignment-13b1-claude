# Downloaded Papers

## Primary Papers

1. **Emergent Misalignment: Narrow finetuning can produce broadly misaligned LLMs** (papers/2502.17424_emergent_misalignment.pdf)
   - Authors: Jan Betley, Daniel Tan, Niels Warncke, Anna Sztyber-Betley, Xuchan Bao, Martin Soto, Nathan Labenz, Owain Evans
   - Year: 2025 (ICML 2025 / Nature 2026)
   - arXiv: 2502.17424
   - Why relevant: PRIMARY paper. Demonstrates emergent misalignment from narrow SFT. Shows MMLU preserved but HumanEval slightly degraded. Provides all training data and evaluation code.

2. **Model Organisms for Emergent Misalignment** (papers/2506.11613_model_organisms_emergent_misalignment.pdf)
   - Authors: Edward Turner, Anna Soligo, Mia Taylor, Senthooran Rajamanoharan, Neel Nanda
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: 2506.11613
   - Why relevant: Improved EM model organisms at smaller scales (0.5B-32B), rank-1 LoRA, phase transitions.

3. **Natural Emergent Misalignment from Reward Hacking** (papers/2511.18397_natural_emergent_misalignment.pdf)
   - Authors: Monte MacDiarmid, Benjamin Wright, et al. (Anthropic)
   - Year: 2025
   - arXiv: 2511.18397
   - Why relevant: Shows EM in realistic RL training, extending beyond SFT.

## Alignment-Capability Tradeoff Papers

4. **Mitigating the Alignment Tax of RLHF** (papers/2309.06256_alignment_tax_rlhf.pdf)

   - Year: 2024 (EMNLP 2024)
   - arXiv: 2309.06256
   - Why relevant: Directly studies alignment tax with capability benchmarks. Model averaging as mitigation.
